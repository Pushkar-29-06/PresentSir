# I6 Faculty Analytics and Proxy Review

Faculty analytics is available at:

```text
/faculty/analytics
```

The workspace provides tabs for:

- Attendance summary
- Trend
- Shortage list
- Early warning
- Proxy review
- Disputes

Proxy items are intentionally presented as **Needs review**. Faculty actions
are `Mark OK`, `Dismiss`, and `Confirm and mark absent`; every action requires
a note of at least five characters. Accepted disputes require a response and
an attendance status, while rejected disputes require a response.
